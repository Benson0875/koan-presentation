# Two Koans, Everyday Life · Group 2

An English, 16-page web presentation about *One Who Is Never Hungry* and *One Who Is Not Sick*. Each course-handout passage appears before its everyday-life film and the group's analysis.

## Open the presentation

Open [index.html](index.html) in a browser, or use the GitHub Pages link in this repository's About section. Use the lower-right button or right arrow key to advance. Use the lower-left link or left arrow key to go back. The cover has a full-screen button. Play each film with its video controls.

The two embedded films are *Three Days to Payday* (2:30) and *Some Were Not* (2:34). They are working cuts with English dialogue and subtitles.

The page starts buffering Film 1 on the cover and Film 2 after Film 1. Keep the report page open while presenting so each video has time to load before it is shown.

## Presenter materials

- [Five-person rehearsal guide](presentation-guide.md): page assignment, brief English cues, and handoff lines.
- [Full English speaking script](full-speaking-script.md): page-by-page rehearsal draft for about 17½ minutes of speaking, in addition to about five minutes of film.

Speaker names and time allocations are intentionally absent from the presentation itself.

For local playback over HTTP, run `python3 -m http.server 8000` in this folder and visit `http://localhost:8000/`.
